# Blind naming test for scenes

Added 16 November 2026, after the owner said of Meadow and Coast: "They don't read clearly and if the title weren't visible it's not clear if a viewer would understand what it is."

**The test.** A reviewer who has read nothing about the project is shown the card at its real size, 350 by 118, with no title, in the Midday and Night palettes. They say what place it is, how sure they are from 1 to 5, which shape told them, and what they could not read.

**The gate.** A scene goes to the illustrator, and then to the owner, only after it scores 5 with the right place as the first reading, and nothing in it is read as an object, an animal or a stain. The illustrator's reviews did not catch this, because the illustrator knew the title. This moves into `CLAUDE.md` with the owner's approval when the branch is merged.

## First run, 16 November 2026

| Scene | First reading | Sure | What told them | What failed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lake A (approved) | Lake with hills | 5 | The mirrored hills and the conifer tips | Nothing |
| Mountains B, Ranges | Misty mountain ridges | 5 | Layered ridgelines and the peak | Nothing |
| Coast B, Headlands | Sea from a headland | 4 | The dark slope | The slope is a flat mass and could be a blot |
| Meadow C, Tall grass | Rolling fields or moor | 3 | The foreground swells | The copse read as an island, a cloud or a stain |
| Coast A, Open sea | Open sea | 3 | The horizon and wave lines | Mostly bands of colour. The foam could be scratches |

The result matches the owner's view. Both scenes that scored 5 have one shape that names the place. Meadow and Coast do not have one yet.

**Status.** Meadow C, Coast A and Coast B: not accepted by the owner, first time. The brief drops a scene that is rejected twice.
