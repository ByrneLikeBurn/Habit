Habit mockups, phase 1

Four screens and a marks board, in three styles and two appearances, then six layouts and flows in Pen and ink. All data is fixture data.

The tab bar, navigation bar and segmented controls are system chrome. They are drawn plain here and the app does not restyle them.

Pen and ink uses New York, and the OS font candidates are Optima and Chalkboard SE. Open this file in Safari on a Mac to see them. The PNGs were rendered on Linux, where Charter stands in for New York and Inter stands in for SF.
