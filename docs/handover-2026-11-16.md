## Lessons from this session

- Ask before acting on a guess about the owner's words. "Densities are off" was guessed and the fix was reverted.
- A reviewer who knows the title cannot tell whether a picture names itself. Use a blind reader.
- A heredoc that fails part way leaves a partial write. Check the state before the commit.
- One decision per message, the result in the first line, and what the owner needs to do next.
